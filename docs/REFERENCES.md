# Selected Primary References

The registry records contain model-specific references. The following works
anchor the architecture-level concepts:

- K. Gödel, “An Example of a New Type of Cosmological Solutions of Einstein's
  Field Equations of Gravitation,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* 21, 447 (1949),
  DOI: `10.1103/RevModPhys.21.447`.
- M. S. Morris, K. S. Thorne and U. Yurtsever, “Wormholes, Time Machines, and
  the Weak Energy Condition,” *Physical Review Letters* 61, 1446 (1988), DOI:
  `10.1103/PhysRevLett.61.1446`.
- D. Deutsch, “Quantum mechanics near closed timelike lines,” *Physical Review
  D* 44, 3197 (1991), DOI: `10.1103/PhysRevD.44.3197`.
- S. W. Hawking, “Chronology protection conjecture,” *Physical Review D* 46,
  603 (1992), DOI: `10.1103/PhysRevD.46.603`.
- E. Minguzzi and M. Sánchez, “The causal hierarchy of spacetimes,”
  `arXiv:gr-qc/0609119`.
- N. E. Rieger, “Causal Classification of Pathological Misner-Type
  Spacetimes,” `arXiv:2501.00841`.
- B.-H. Lee, N. A. Nilsson and S. Thakur, “There and back again—Closed
  timelike curves as EFT selection principle,” `arXiv:2602.17724`.

A registry entry must cite the source of its metric and conventions. Inclusion
in CTC-FA does not transfer authorship of the underlying theory or solution.
